# Move and resize

Moment windows can be moved and resized freely within the usable screen area.

## Move

Press and drag the Moment bottom control bar to move the window. The system constrains the
final position so the window and its controls remain accessible.

## Resize

Drag any corner of a Moment to resize it continuously. The new scale and position are
kept when you release.

When the window reaches its minimum size and resizing ends, it enters compact mode. The
maximum scale may be limited by the screen orientation and available area.

App content scales together with the window Surface; the Activity is not recreated for
each drag.
