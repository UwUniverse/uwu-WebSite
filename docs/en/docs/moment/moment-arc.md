# MomentArc

MomentArc is an arc-shaped quick launcher invoked from a bottom screen corner. Once
enabled, swipe inward and upward from the lower-left or lower-right corner to open the
corresponding MomentArc.

As your finger moves between icons, the current target is highlighted. Release to start
the selected app or shortcut as a Moment. Cancelling the gesture does not launch anything.

## Customize the layout

Moment settings provide a MomentArc layout editor.

- The left and right sides have independent layouts.
- Apps and app shortcuts can be added.
- Apps and shortcuts can be searched.
- Tap or drag an item to a target position.
- Long-press an occupied position to clear it.
- The “More apps” position opens the complete app list.

MomentArc currently responds only to corner gestures on the default display.
