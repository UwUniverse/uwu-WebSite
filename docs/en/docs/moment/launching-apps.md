# Launch apps

Moment settings provide a “Launch apps as Moment” entry. It shows a list of launchable
apps; tap an app icon to open it directly as a Moment.

- The app list is sorted by name.
- Launching creates an independent Moment task without opening the app fullscreen first.
- Apps without a launchable Activity do not appear in the list.
- If an app rejects the launch or its entry is no longer valid, the system reports that it cannot be opened.

> Tip: Double-tap the navigation bar on the home screen to open the Moment app list directly.
